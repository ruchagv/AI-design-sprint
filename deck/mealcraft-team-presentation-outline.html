Section 2 · Problem
Slide 02
Meet Karan
⏱ 2 min
Make the problem human before making it a product. The team should feel the pain before seeing the solution.
Talk track
  • Rucha "Karan is 28, lives alone in Bengaluru, has a cook who arrives at 8am every weekday."
  • "Every morning — barely awake — he has to answer: what should I tell her to make?"
  • "He has no answer. He repeats yesterday's meal, or just says 'make whatever's there.'"
  • "By evening he's on Swiggy for 20 minutes ordering something he'll regret."
  • "He's tried HealthifyMe. He quit after 3 weeks — logging felt like homework."
Visuals
📱 Persona card 🗓 Typical morning timeline 💬 Quote: "Make whatever's there"
Slide 03
The Real Problem
⏱ 3 min
Elevate from a surface frustration to the underlying systems problem.
Talk track
  • Jyotish "The problem is not lack of knowledge — Karan knows what he wants to eat."
  • "The problem is no system converts what he already knows about himself into an answer waiting for him at the right moment."
  • "Existing apps solve the wrong problem — they ask him to log, track, and decide every day. That is the behaviour that is failing him."
  • "Our design principle: the decision must already be made before Karan has to make it."

Section 3 · Solution
Slide 04
How MealCraft Works
⏱ 3 min
Explain the core mechanic in plain language before showing any UI.
Talk track
  • Rucha "Karan sets up his preferences once — diet, cuisine, health goal, cook schedule. 5 minutes. Never again."
  • "Every night MealCraft plans the next day's meals silently in the background."
  • "At 7:15am — before his cook arrives — he gets a notification: Breakfast: Poha · Lunch: Dal chawal."
  • "He taps. The app has already generated a Hindi voice note for his cook."
  • "He sends it to Savitri on WhatsApp. Done in under 10 seconds."
  • "He doesn't log anything. He doesn't rate anything. The app is silent for the rest of the day."
Visual: 3-step flow — Set up once → Morning notification → Tap to send
Slide 05
What Makes It Different
⏱ 2 min
Three sharp differentiators. Quick. Don't over-explain.
Talk track
  • Jyotish "No logging — ever. Not even on Day 1. The product works passively."
  • "No daily decisions — the meal is planned before you wake up. You react, you don't decide."
  • "Cook-first output — not a meal plan PDF. A Hindi voice note ready to send to your cook on WhatsApp."

Live Demo — Walkthrough
⏱ 10 min
Walk the team through all 7 flows in the prototype. Use the nav panel to jump between screens cleanly.
Demo script — in order
  • Rucha Splash + Value Prop — "This is the first thing Karan sees. No login wall — we defer that entirely."

Section 5 · Key Design Decisions
Slide 07
5 Decisions to Align On
⏱ 5 min
Surface the decisions that need the team's input — not things already resolved.
Open questions
  • Hindi voice note vs text message — Confident in TTS quality for Private Preview, or ship text-only first?
  • No evening engagement — No end-of-day prompts or ratings. Right call even though it limits feedback signals?
  • Android share sheet model — No WhatsApp Business API. Karan manually completes the send in WhatsApp. Acceptable for V1?
  • One-time onboarding, never repeated — Preferences editable in Settings but never re-prompted. Does this hold if meal quality degrades over time?
  • Week view as secondary surface — Day View is always home. Is there a case for making Week View the default for power users?
