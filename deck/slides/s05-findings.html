WHAT WE FOUND

8 pain points. The loudest ones.

★★★★ HIGHEST FREQUENCY

Decision fatigue when ordering

Scrolling Swiggy while hungry leads to paralysis and regret.

"Takes a lot of scrolling... getting hangry because I can't decide."

Mental load of the cook instruction

Early morning, half-awake, cook waiting — nothing to say.

"Mostly in the mornings. To put my mind into it... is a struggle."

★★★ HIGH FREQUENCY

Taste vs. health conflict

Constant internal war between what they want and what they should eat.

"I just end up eating healthy, not-so-tasty food or unhealthy food."

Food repetition and boredom

Eating the same 3 dishes weekly — not by choice, but by inertia.

"Not because you like it very much. Because it's always convenient."

★★★ HIGH FREQUENCY

Can't follow diet plans

Every app and nutritionist plan abandoned within 3 weeks.

"I wasn't able to follow through it after like 3 weeks."

Manual tracking too effortful

Daily logging feels like a chore — becomes another thing to fail at.

"I would have to put a reminder to track this. Which is like more work."
