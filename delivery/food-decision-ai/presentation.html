The User

Meet Karan

28 · Bengaluru · Cook arrives at 8am
Every morning
Barely awake, has to answer:
"What should I tell her to make?"
What he does instead
Repeats yesterday's meal or says "make whatever's there"
By evening
20 minutes on Swiggy, orders something he'll regret
Has tried
HealthifyMe — quit after 3 weeks. "Logging felt like homework."
"Make whatever's there."
— Karan, every weekday morning
The Problem
Karan knows what he wants to eat.
He has no system that converts what he knows
into an answer waiting for him.
Existing apps fail because
  • They ask him to log every meal
  • They require daily decisions
  • They treat him as a patient, not a professional
Our design principle
  • Decision made before he wakes up
  • Zero daily input after onboarding
  • Cook handoff costs him nothing

Sprint Learnings

What we learned building this

AI design sprint · key takeaways
📋
Prioritise epics early
It shapes everything downstream — scope, prototype depth, and what gets cut. Without it, the sprint drifts.
⚙️
Understand the backend foundations
Core backend elements such as data models, CRM systems, APIs, integrations, business logic, and infrastructure shape what can be designed and delivered.
🐙
GitHub makes collaboration real
One repo, one source of truth. Work in parallel, review changes, nothing gets lost or overwritten.
💬
Bob is strong at synthesising conversations
Hours of notes, threads, and research — turned into structured outputs in minutes.
💡
AI accelerates ideation
Generating solution concepts and design directions takes minutes instead of days. AI expands the option space — the team decides what to pursue.
🧠
Bob's memory changes the workflow
Context persists across sessions. The sprint builds on itself — no repeated briefings, no lost decisions.
🎨
Bob is strong at visual design, needs human review on detail
Layouts, colour, and structure come out well. Spacing, edge cases, and accessibility still need a human pass.
📝
Use AI for the first draft, not the final word
AI gets you to 70% fast. The last 30% — nuance, tone, judgment — is yours.
