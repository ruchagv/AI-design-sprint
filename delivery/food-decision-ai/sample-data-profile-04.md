## 7. Recipe View — "I'll cook this" (Monday Breakfast: Aloo Paratha)

> Shown when Meera taps "I'll cook this" on the Breakfast row.

```
Aloo Paratha

🏷  North Indian · Filling
🎯  General wellness–friendly
⏱  25 minutes
👥  Serves 3–4

Ingredients
───────────
• 2 cups whole wheat flour
• 3 medium potatoes, boiled and mashed
• 1 tsp cumin seeds
• ½ tsp red chilli powder (mild)
• ½ tsp coriander powder
• Salt to taste
• 2 tbsp oil or ghee

Method
───────────
1. Mix flour with water to form a smooth, soft dough. Rest for 10 minutes.
2. Combine mashed potatoes with cumin, chilli powder, coriander, and salt.
3. Divide dough into equal balls. Flatten each, fill with a spoonful of potato mix.
4. Seal and roll out gently into a round paratha.
5. Cook on a hot tawa with a little oil or ghee until golden on both sides.
6. Serve hot with curd.
```
