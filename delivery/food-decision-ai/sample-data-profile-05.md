## 7. Recipe View — "I'll cook this" (Monday Breakfast: Chicken Fried Egg Roll)

> Shown when Rohan taps "I'll cook this" on the Breakfast row.

```
Chicken Fried Egg Roll

🏷  Chinese · Quick
🎯  No specific goal
⏱  15 minutes
👥  Serves 1

Ingredients
───────────
• 2 eggs, beaten
• ½ cup shredded rotisserie chicken (or leftover)
• 2 tbsp spring onion, chopped
• 1 tsp soy sauce
• ½ tsp chilli sauce
• Salt and pepper to taste
• 1 tsp oil
• 1 roti or tortilla wrap

Method
───────────
1. Heat oil in a pan. Pour beaten eggs and scramble lightly until just set.
2. Add shredded chicken, spring onion, soy sauce, and chilli sauce.
3. Stir-fry for 1 minute until combined and heated through.
4. Spoon filling onto a roti. Roll tightly. Serve immediately.
```
